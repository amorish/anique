Synopsis

In the year 2142, humanity has retreated into subterranean mega-structures following the "Crimson Cascade" event. When anomalous signals begin infiltrating the neural-networks of the lower sectors, elite pilot Ren Ikari is awakened from cryo-stasis. Tasked with piloting the experimental prototype unit, he must navigate a fragmented reality where memories are manufactured and the line between human consciousness and artificial singularity blurs completely. The fight for the remnants of earth has begun.
